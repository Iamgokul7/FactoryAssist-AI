How it answers

Retrieval-augmented generation over controlled documents.

  1. 1
    Your question is embedded

    Sent to the Gemini embedding model configured for this deployment.

  2. 2
    Active revisions are searched

    Only chunks from current documents are retrieved; superseded revisions stay out.

  3. 3
    The evidence is passed to Gemini

    The model is instructed to answer only from those extracts.

  4. 4
    Sources are returned

    Document title, code, revision and page for every citation.

Known limitations

Stated plainly, so the tool is not trusted beyond what it does.

  • Answers are limited to the indexed documents and their current revisions.
  • Scanned pages without a text layer are reported as extraction warnings, not read with OCR.
  • Tables are extracted as plain text, so very wide tables may read awkwardly.
  • The assistant refuses when evidence is weak; it is not a substitute for the controlled SOP or the responsible supervisor.
  • Runs locally on the plant network; no authentication is implemented in this build.
